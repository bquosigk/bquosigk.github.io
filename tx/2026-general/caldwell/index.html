Caldwell County, Texas: 2026 Voter Guide

✅ Checked against the official county sample ballot (Oct 19, 2026). Coverage confirmed: the county's Publication Ballot lists every statewide and local contest in the joint election; it matches the county's 13 area ballots and the posted orders of election. Which local contests you see depends on where you live. Lockhart ISD uses cumulative voting for its three at-large seats: you have three votes and may give them to one candidate or split them, so each name is printed three times on the ballot. Ballot error: the sample ballot prints Justice of the Peace Precinct 4 candidate Yvette M. Mireles as (REP); she won the Democratic primary (county's official March 3, 2026 results) and is certified as a Democrat, which is what this page shows. Cancelled (unopposed candidates declared elected): City of Mustang Ridge, City of Niederwald, Maxwell Special Utility District, Edwards Aquifer Authority.
