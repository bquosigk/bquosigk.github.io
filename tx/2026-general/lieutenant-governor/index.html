    We in Texas have a transportation system that is designed for motor vehicles. In practical terms, a Texan must maintain a motor vehicle in order to drive to the places they need to go. Our transportation system is very expensive, inefficient, unsafe, and increasingly uneconomic.

    To serve the public interest the transportation system must consist of a variety of modes — motor vehicles, bus systems, bicycle tracks, pedestrian walkways, and rail systems. Each of these modes competes for the same rights of way and access space, and the same transportation infrastructure dollars. …

    I propose that we allocate 10% of state transportation spending to increasing the urban bus fleet with the objective of providing commuter transit and high frequency bus routes, and also to building safe bicycle tracks with practical, useful routes.
