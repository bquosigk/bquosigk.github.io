Ballot questions

  • TSPLOST (Fulton outside Atlanta): Additional 0.75% sales tax in Fulton County outside the City of Atlanta for up to five years for transportation improvements and congestion reduction.
  • ESPLOST (Fulton County Schools & Atlanta Public Schools): Reimpose the 1% education sales tax from July 1, 2027 for up to 20 quarters, raising up to $2,095,680,577 for school construction, renovation, technology, safety, equipment and buses; includes up to $350M in APS general obligation debt.
  • City of Sandy Springs Redevelopment Powers: Authorize Sandy Springs to use powers under the Redevelopment Powers Law.
  • City of Palmetto Ad Valorem Tax Exemption: 100% city property tax exemption on primary residences for Palmetto residents aged 65+ by Jan 1, 2026.
