Ballot questions

  • City of Corpus Christi, Proposition A (Continuation of Corpus Christi Crime Control and Prevention District): Whether the Corpus Christi Crime Control and Prevention District should be continued for ten years and the crime control and prevention district sales tax should be continued for ten years. (Full ballot language: official sample ballot, page 3.)
  • City of Corpus Christi, Proposition B (City of Corpus Christi Charter Amendment Regarding Industrial Water Drought Surcharge and Curtailment): Shall Article IX for the City Charter be amended to terminate and prohibit programs that exempt large industrial water users from drought surcharges or curtailment requirements during declared drought stages, and require large industrial water users to pay drought surcharges at rates set by the City Council in the March 2025 Drought Contingency Plan? (Full ballot language: official sample ballot, page 3.)
  • Nueces County Emergency Services District No. 1, Proposition A: THIS IS A TAX INCREASE The adoption of a local sales and use tax in Nueces County Emergency Services District No. 1 at a rate not to exceed two percent (2%) in any location in the District. (Full ballot language: official sample ballot, page 4.)
  • West Oso Independent School District, Proposition A (VATRE): THIS IS A TAX INCREASE Ratifying the ad valorem tax rate of $1.2169 in West Oso Independent School District for the current year, a rate that will result in an increase of 8.95 percent in maintenance and operations tax revenue for the district for the current year as compared to the preceding year, which is an additional $2,039,356. (Full ballot language: official sample ballot, page 3.)
