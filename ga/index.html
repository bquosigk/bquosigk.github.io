Georgia elections

The Nov. 3, 2026 general election has been held. Unofficial statewide results and registration counts are below. A Dec. 1 runoff, if one is needed, is still listed as coming soon.

Registered voters

Georgia does not register voters by party, so there is no Democrat or Republican count of the rolls. The U.S. Election Assistance Commission says, “Update Political Party: Georgia does not have party registration.” Gwinnett County Elections says that because Georgia has an open primary, voters are “not required to register a party affiliation” and instead request a Democratic, Republican, or nonpartisan ballot.

  • Active registered: 7,530,664 as of 2026-10-30, early Friday morning Eastern (America/New_York), just before Election Day. CBS News Atlanta, citing the Georgia Secretary of State’s Election Data Hub
  • Total registered: 8,226,389 as of 2026-10-30, 11:35 a.m. ET (America/New_York). WALB, citing unofficial Georgia Secretary of State data

These counts are what CBS News Atlanta and WALB reported from Secretary of State figures on Oct. 30, 2026, not a live scrape of the SOS Election Data Hub (that page returned a Cloudflare block when checked). Inactive registrants remain registered and may vote. Because Georgia has no party registration, there is no official Democrat/Republican breakdown.
