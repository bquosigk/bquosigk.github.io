Ballot questions

  • Statewide Amendment 1 – Conservation use property acreage: Raise the max acreage eligible for bona fide conservation use assessment from 2,000 to 4,000 acres.
  • Statewide Amendment 2 – Nonpartisan probate judge elections: Require probate judges to be elected in nonpartisan elections.
  • Statewide Amendment 3 – Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund: Authorize a dedicated fund for 9-1-1 system expansion, maintenance and operation.
  • Bartow County Homestead Exemption (HB 1491): Increase the county homestead exemption from $15,000 to $30,000 of assessed value (county ad valorem taxes).
  • City of Cartersville Homestead Exemption (HB 1321): City homestead exemption of $100,000 of assessed value (city residents only).
  • City of White – Sunday sales by the drink; Sunday package sales: Two local referendums (City of White residents only).

City by city

  • Euharlee: City Council special election (unexpired term of the late Joe Turner) — 3 candidates, contested (see race above).
  • Cartersville: Homestead exemption referendum (HB 1321, Act 557): $100,000 of assessed value from city ad valorem taxes. No city or Cartersville City Schools board races.
  • White: Two referendums: Sunday sales by the drink; Sunday package sales (malt beverages, wine, distilled spirits, 12:30–11:30 PM). No candidate races.
  • Adairsville: No city races or questions on the Nov 3 ballot.
  • Emerson: No city races or questions on the Nov 3 ballot.
  • Kingston: No city races or questions on the Nov 3 ballot.
  • Taylorsville: No city races or questions on the Nov 3 ballot.
