Methodology

  • Candidate positions are verbatim excerpts from campaign websites, Ballotpedia Candidate Connection surveys, news reports or labeled social posts. A build check confirms that each excerpt exactly matches the saved source text.
  • Scorecard rows come from Faith & Freedom Coalition voter guides (the Georgia affiliate's cards in Georgia; the national FFC cards in Texas). They are FFC's own advocacy publications; we show FFC's wording, ratings and footnotes as published.
  • Labels show the type of each item: Campaign, Survey, News (a journalist's description) or Social media. If no public statement was found, the card says “No public positions found.” Nothing is guessed.
  • Donors: federal candidates use the Federal Election Commission's bulk data files (itemized receipts of the candidate's principal campaign committee, 2025–2026 cycle) and FEC summary totals; the as-of date is shown on each page. Memo entries are excluded so nothing is counted twice. State and local candidates link to the state's official campaign-finance search until those filings are loaded. No figures are estimated.
  • Elections: every page lives under its election (for example /ga/2026-general/). Primaries and special elections will get their own sections; older addresses redirect to the current page.
  • Georgia: local races were checked against official county sample ballots. Texas: partisan races come from the Texas Secretary of State's official 2026 Ballot Certification; each county page says whether its nonpartisan local items have been checked.
  • This site has no advertising and no paid placement.
